Changes made by Nuvio: when installed, the shader gets explicit GLSL precision qualifiers for mobile GPUs (medium precision where full precision is not needed), and its convolution passes read each neighbouring pixel once instead of twice. Upscalers also get a second copy whose //!WHEN scale threshold is lowered to 1.0 for the "Apply always" option. Nothing else is changed.

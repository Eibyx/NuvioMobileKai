NVIDIA Image Scaling v1.0.2 by NVIDIA
ported to mpv by agyild

Changelog
Made it directly operate on LUMA plane, since the original shader was operating
on LUMA by deriving it from RGB. This should cause a major increase in performance,
especially on OpenGL 4.0+ renderers

Changes made by Nuvio: when installed, the shader gets explicit GLSL precision qualifiers for mobile GPUs (medium precision where full precision is not needed). Upscalers also get a second copy whose //!WHEN scale threshold is lowered to 1.0 for the "Apply always" option. Nothing else is changed.
